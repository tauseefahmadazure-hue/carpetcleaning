How it works

Five checkpoints, one flawless finish

Every job follows the same disciplined sequence — it's how we guarantee consistent, professional carpet cleaning results every time.

  1. 01

    Inspection

    We assess fibre type, soil level and problem areas to plan the right approach for your carpet.

  2. 02

    Deep Cleaning

    Hot water extraction and targeted pre-treatments lift dirt from deep within the pile.

  3. 03

    Sanitisation

    Eco-friendly sanitising agents neutralise bacteria, allergens and odours at the source.

  4. 04

    Drying

    High-velocity air movers speed up drying so most carpets are ready to walk on within hours.

  5. 05

    Final Quality Check

    A final walk-through with you confirms every area meets our 100% satisfaction standard.

Carpet cleaning FAQs

Most single rooms range from £45–£65, while whole-home packages typically fall between £89–£149 depending on square footage, soil level and the number of rooms. We provide a fixed, no-obligation quote before any work begins.

With our low-moisture hot water extraction system, most carpets are dry to the touch within 2–4 hours and fully cured within 6–8 hours, helped along by our high-velocity air movers.

Yes. Our eco-friendly carpet cleaning solutions are non-toxic, biodegradable and free from harsh chemical residue, so your family and pets can safely return to the room as soon as it's dry.

In most London postcodes we can offer same-day carpet cleaning when you book before midday, subject to technician availability. Emergency and next-day slots are also available for urgent stain or flood situations.

Our technicians are trained in targeted stain removal for wine, coffee, pet accidents, ink and grease. While no company can guarantee 100% removal of every set-in stain, our pre-treatment process resolves the vast majority of cases.

Steam cleaning (hot water extraction) flushes deep-down dirt and allergens out of the pile and is our recommended method for most homes. Carpet shampooing works a foaming solution into the surface fibres and suits heavily soiled commercial carpet. We advise on the right method during your free quote.

Yes, alongside carpet and rug cleaning we offer full upholstery cleaning for sofas, dining chairs and curtains, plus mattress cleaning and sanitisation using fabric-safe, low-moisture methods.

We work with offices, hotels, restaurants and retail units across London, offering flexible out-of-hours commercial carpet cleaning contracts designed to keep disruption to your business to a minimum.

For most households we recommend professional deep carpet cleaning every 6–12 months. High-traffic homes, homes with pets or young children, and commercial spaces often benefit from cleaning every 3–6 months.

Yes, Pile & Co. is fully insured and every technician holds recognised carpet and upholstery cleaning certifications, so your property is in trained, accountable hands.
